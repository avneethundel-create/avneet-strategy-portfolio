Overview

Every sales call is market research. Most teams throw it away.

I was the one making the calls at Firmable. The same objections, competitor names and buyer language came up again and again, but none of it was captured anywhere. So I built a system that turns call transcripts into objection guides, ICP frameworks and battlecards, and I reviewed every output before reps saw it.

Hard to sell segments

Some verticals buy slowly no matter how good the pitch is. For these, I moved reps from cold calls to multi threaded account plays. Map three or more stakeholders, lead with a peer reference in the same industry, and use a short personalised Loom for the senior decision maker.

How it's built

Process first, then the tool

Every automation here started with a sales process I mapped by hand. The AI only does the parts that were repetitive. A person still decides what reps see.

Principles

Map the process by hand first

If I can't draw the workflow on paper, automating it just makes the mess faster.

Keep a person in the loop

AI drafts. I review. Reps only see guidance that a human has checked against real calls.

Ship where reps already work

Outputs live in HubSpot, Notion and the inbox. Nobody needs a new tool to use them.
